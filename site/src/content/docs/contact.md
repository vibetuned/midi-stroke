Midi Stroke is made in the open, and every report helps.

- **Report a bug or suggest an idea:**
  [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/vibetuned/midi-stroke/issues).
- **Write to us:** [dev@vibetuned.com](mailto:dev@vibetuned.com).

## Reporting a bug

A few details make a bug much quicker to find:

- the version of the app, and whether it's the desktop app or the browser (which one);
- your system: macOS, Windows or Linux, and its version;
- your MIDI device, if it's involved;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

## Credits and removals

The app uses scores, samples and sounds made by others, credited with the files they came in.
If you made one of them and would like it credited differently, or removed, write to
[dev@vibetuned.com](mailto:dev@vibetuned.com) and it will be done promptly.
