Midi Stroke connects your MIDI instrument to professionally engraved sheet
music (Verovio + MEI) and scores what you play, note by note. Two modes on
every piece: **Rhythm** — play along in time, scored with hit/miss windows —
and **Practice** — playback pauses on each note and waits until you press the
right one.

## Bring your own scores

Load a single MEI file, or import a whole **ZIP of MEI scores** as a new
collection stored permanently on your device — no server needed. Scores
don't need any special preparation: the app adds its one-beat count-in
measure automatically. [Songs & importing →](songs/)
