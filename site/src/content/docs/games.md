The **Games** tab has short games about timing, and they need no instrument: a key, a tap on the
screen or a drum pad will do. They're for anyone, whether or not you play yet. Every level is real
rhythm, and the songs are real pieces you can go on to play.

## Slingshot

Hold to orbit, let go to fling. Press and hold **space** (or touch, or any MIDI key) as the probe
reaches an anchor, and it whips around it, a quarter turn every beat. Let go at the white mark and
it's flung to the next anchor. How long you hold *is* the note: a half note is half a circle, a
whole note a full one, and the note sounds for exactly as long as you hold it. Each anchor shows
the note written out (an open whole or half note, a filled quarter, flagged eighths, dotted
values). The part of the orbit still to go glows up to the mark where you let go, and shrinks as
you hold, so you always know where you're going and where to stop.

- **Lessons** teach one idea each: half notes, quarter notes, whole notes, a waltz in 3/4, eighth
  notes, dotted rhythms.
- **Songs** are the melodies of well-known tunes from the library: *Twinkle Twinkle*, *Ode to Joy*,
  *Scarborough Fair* and more. Finish one and **Play it on the saxophone →** opens it in the saxo
  app, to learn the notes as well as the rhythm.
- **My pieces:** add any MEI score with **📁 Add a piece**, and its top line becomes a level. It's
  kept on your device, and it also appears in the piano app under *My pieces*, where the button
  after a run takes you.
- **Speed:** *Relaxed* plays at three quarters of the tempo; *As written* plays at the piece's own.

After each run you see how it went, with stars, accuracy, and a tip when you tend to let go early
or rush. You also see **the rhythm written out, every note coloured by how you played it**.

## Timing calibration

Every device takes a moment to play a sound and to notice a key. Choose **⏱ Calibrate timing**
once and tap along to eight clicks. The games then take that moment off, so a hit on the beat
counts as on the beat.
