The theory app is a **train & practice course player**: each course module
pairs lesson videos with worksheet exercises rendered as real engraved
scores. Instead of playing along with a transport, you *write* music.

![Theory app](/screenshots/theory.png)

## How it works

- **Courses** (Elementary rudiments, Notation, …) are organized in modules;
  each module has videos and exercises, with progress and watched-video
  tracking kept locally.
- **Exercises are fill-in-the-blank scores**: interval ear-tests, primary
  triads and inversions, cadence writing, passing and auxiliary notes,
  dominant sevenths and more.
- **Answer from any input**: the clickable virtual piano, the
  circle-of-fifths wheel, or your MIDI keyboard.
- **Check, reveal, listen** — verify your answer, reveal the model solution,
  or play back either one.

No MIDI instrument is required — theory is fully playable with mouse or
touch.
