*Last updated: 20 October 2026.*

Midi Stroke — in the browser, the desktop app and the Android and iOS apps —
**does not collect, store or share any personal data**. It has no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Vibetuned, a non-profit for music and
arts education, makes it.

## What stays on your device

What you do in the app stays on your device and is never sent anywhere: your
statistics and results, your settings, the scores you import, your keyboard's
range and your drum kit's pad map. It is deleted when you clear the app's data,
or uninstall the app.

## What the app connects to

- **Piano sounds**, downloaded from `tonejs.github.io` (GitHub Pages) the first
  time they are needed.
- **The theory lessons' videos**, played from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced
  mode (`youtube-nocookie.com`), only when you open a lesson.
- **The web app and this site**, served by GitHub Pages; the apps carry their
  own files.
- **Links** you open, in your browser.

Those services receive the requests your device makes to them — like any web
page you visit — under their own privacy policies:
[GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement),
[Google / YouTube](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## MIDI and Bluetooth

The app listens to your MIDI instrument to score your playing, on your device.
On Android and iOS, Bluetooth is used only to find and connect the MIDI
instrument you choose; the Android app asks for the *nearby devices* permission
for that, and never uses your location.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.
